Architectural Conclusion:
-----------------------------

Metadata filtering is an effective retrieval optimization when reliable and well-defined metadata boundaries are available. By restricting vector search to a relevant subset of the knowledge base, it can significantly reduce the retrieval search space and potentially improve retrieval efficiency and latency. However, overly restrictive filters can exclude relevant knowledge and reduce recall, as observed in this experiment. Therefore, metadata filtering should be used as a complementary constraint to semantic retrieval, particularly where strong business boundaries such as network element, product, release, or alarm domain are available.
